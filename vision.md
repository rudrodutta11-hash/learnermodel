# Vision

> **We do not model what a user knows. We model HOW a user learns.**

## The Problem

Every existing education platform focuses on curriculum. We focus on the
learner. This is not another AI language learning app — it is what we
believe is the future of education.

## The Core Idea

The product continuously learns:

- how quickly a user forgets information
- what teaching methods work best for them
- how long they can focus
- how confident they are (and how well-calibrated that confidence is)
- whether stories, conversations, quizzes, writing, flashcards or videos work best
- what mistakes they repeatedly make
- how much challenge keeps them engaged

Over time the AI becomes a better teacher *specifically for that individual*.

## The Learner Model is Universal

The learner model works across every subject. If someone learns Spanish
for six months and then starts History, the AI already understands how
they learn. It does NOT need to relearn the person.

**The learner model is universal. Subjects simply consume it.**

## We Are Not an AI Language Company

Language is simply the first proving ground. The long-term vision is an
adaptive AI teacher for every subject:

- Languages
- Engineering
- Medicine
- History
- Finance
- Programming
- Music

## The One Question

The recommendation engine always answers one question:

> **"What is the highest impact thing this learner can do in the next few minutes?"**

The learner never has to choose a lesson. The AI chooses. The learner
simply opens the app.

The app understands:

- available time
- learner profile
- goals
- past mistakes
- confidence
- memory state

…and recommends the best experience.

## Everything Else is Delivery

Calls, videos, quizzes, writing, flashcards, stories, games — these are
delivery mechanisms, not products. **The recommendation engine and
learner model are the core product.**

Every engineering decision should reinforce this philosophy.
